How It Works

How a project works

A stress-free remodeling process.

1. Free consultation

We visit your home, see your space and talk about your goals.

2. Price range

You get an estimated range first, because selections affect the final cost.

3. Design and selections

Measurements, design concepts and product selections, then we review the contract together.

4. Production

Scheduling, site preparation, demolition and coordination of all materials, trades and timelines.

Our Story

A founder who knows the trade

Donald Robbins started Robbins Construction in 2004 after almost 30 years in the home improvement business in different roles. He holds an accounting degree from Harding University and a CPA license, and says his favorite part of the work is seeing the change in the homes the company touches and how happy its customers are.
